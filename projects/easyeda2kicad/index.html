EasyEDA to KiCad Converter

Most parts I buy for JLCPCB assembly come from LCSC, but I design in KiCad. The upstream easyeda2kicad by uPesy handles the conversion — it pulls EasyEDA's data and writes KiCad libraries.

My fork exists, but it's barely a fork. My only commit on it (c639590) changes a newline in requirements.txt. The User-Agent/Referer header fix people sometimes credit to me is actually Steffen Wittemeier's, in upstream commit 95e3e0e.

The real work lives in gs-kicad-lib, which depends on easyeda2kicad>=1.0.1 and wraps it in the workflow I actually use — interactive importer with fuzzy library and footprint search, generated/existing/no footprint link options, field and procurement metadata normalization, required-field validation, and setup tooling that registers everything with KiCad in one shot.
