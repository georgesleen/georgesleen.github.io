# Revisions

(Not) getting things perfect the first time and trying again.

Originally inspired by my [microcouple](../../projects/microcouple) project,
\where I took my usual tendency to try making a perfect first revision to the
extreme and ended up spending a lot of money on a pretty much non-functional
board that I couldn't learn anything from. It got me thinking about how often
this pattern appears in my life. Projects, hobbies, relationships, I find it hard to think about something in my life in which I haven't expected myself to excel at first and then fail some degree of spectacularly.
